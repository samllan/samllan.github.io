Can an arrival list shorten the reading room queue?

This fictional pilot shows how the same logbook can track a different project. A small library wants to help booked visitors get seated sooner.

  1. Compare the two sign-in methods

    Record the wait from arrival to seating under both methods.

  2. Decide what to test next

    Keep the outcome, the reason for the decision, and the next measurement together.

Review the decision

The shorter wait is promising enough to justify a better comparison.

What I tried
Compare the medians with the session notes and check what the trial controlled.
What I found
The arrival list ran in the afternoon and the paper list ran in the morning. Time of day may explain the difference.
Decision or next step
Alternate sign-in methods in matching time slots and record both visitor and staff time.
