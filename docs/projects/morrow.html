Morrow Plan

Record thoughts and find them again later.

Capture a voice note before the thought disappears. Keep recordings, words and photos together, and return to them through a calendar or a persistent collection.

The aim

Make reflection easy to begin and useful to revisit.

The work

I conceived the product and directed its design and AI-assisted implementation, contributing code and refining the experience through repeated visual and functional feedback.

What exists

The current project supports guest use, local persistence, recording and playback, calendar organisation and optional account sync. The core design balances quick capture with finding earlier entries.

What I learnt

I wanted recording a thought to be quick, with dates and collections helping people find it later.
