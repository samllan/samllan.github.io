The shape of a season

Use a repeating model for a repeating year.

At the AA, I used Fourier techniques to model seasonal weather patterns. A year repeats; a seasonal model should be able to join December back to January naturally.

One year. Several waves.

Illustrative · synthetic data
Example seasonal shape Approximation

Add harmonics to capture more of this constructed seasonal shape. This demonstrates representation, not forecast accuracy.

The aim

Represent the broad seasonal curve while allowing its shape to depart from a single, perfectly symmetric sine wave.

The work

I applied a sum of periodic sine and cosine components to seasonal weather modelling. Additional harmonics can represent asymmetry and finer structure without losing the annual cycle.

What exists

The project is part of my operational analytics work at the AA. The interactive figure explains the modelling idea using synthetic data; it is not an AA dataset or a recovered project result.

The useful insight

A flexible fit is not automatically a better forecast. The number of harmonics should be chosen with validation; it can otherwise fit noise.
