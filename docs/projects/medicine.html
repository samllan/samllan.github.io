Medical education · Hackathon prototype

Incidental AI

A clinical scenario becomes a learning conversation.

A hackathon exploration of generative AI for medical teaching: present a simulated clinical scenario and image, invite a learner to reason about it, and respond conversationally.

ScenarioGenerated teaching case
ReasoningLearner interpretation
DiscussionConversational feedback

The aim

Explore an interactive way to practise clinical reasoning with generated teaching material.

The work

Built together in a four-person hackathon team, without AI-assisted coding. The prototype combines a Streamlit interface, scenario generation, image-generation calls and a chat component.

What exists

The archive contains application code and a recorded Streamlit demonstration from September 2023. The code also contains fallback paths and unfinished sections; it is presented as a hackathon concept.

The useful insight

A convincing teaching interface needs reliable, reviewed examples and feedback. Generated scenarios and images do not establish clinical correctness.
