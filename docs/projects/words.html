St Andrews · Interactive visualisation

A map of words

Follow meanings. Discover connections.

A visual way to explore how words relate. Search for a word, follow its synonyms, inspect alternative meanings and retrace the route you took.

The aim

Make relationships between words visible and explorable, rather than presenting another flat list of definitions.

The work

An equal collaboration with Nina Sushko at St Andrews: Nina designed the interface, and I built the underlying data and application logic. The implementation combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript and D3.

What exists

The prototype explores a subset of 484 words. Distance represents shared synonym relationships; size communicates frequency and precision. Search and graph rendering have been checked on the live site.

The useful insight

A network can turn looking up one word into discovering a neighbourhood of related meanings. It represents synonym relationships, not a historical family tree.
