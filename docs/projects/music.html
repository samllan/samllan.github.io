Music Note Rain

Falling piano notes with synchronised playback.

A piano-note waterfall that turns musical timing into something you can see. The local prototype explores lead, accompaniment and combined arrangements at five levels of complexity.

First Light

Composed demo · from the existing prototype
24-second practice pattern · synthesised sound

The aim

Make a playable musical outline easier to follow, with the sound and falling notes telling the same story.

The work

I developed the idea and directed its AI-assisted implementation. The prototype combines audio analysis, constrained musical arrangements, note visualisation and local storage.

What exists

The local prototype supports imported recordings, three arrangement types and five difficulty levels. This browser excerpt plays its existing composed demo; it does not analyse uploaded music.

What I learnt

A useful arrangement preserves the musical phrase while reducing the number of gestures a player must perform. Mixed-recording transcription remains experimental.
