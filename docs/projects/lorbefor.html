Lorbefor

Make nearby possibilities easier to act on.

A map-based product for finding local activities, organising events and connecting around shared interests.

The aim

Help people move from “what could I do nearby?” to a concrete plan.

The work

I conceived the product and directed its design and AI-assisted development, contributing code and shaping the map, event and account experiences.

What exists

A web product with map-based discovery, events, attendance and groups. The development work includes account handling and fixes to the real published interface.

The useful insight

The map needs to lead to a useful next action: discover something, inspect it, then take part.
