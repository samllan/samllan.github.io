Strathclyde boat challenge

Built together. Designed to go further.

A hands-on boat-building challenge at Strathclyde became a formative experience in practical design and working as a team. The aim was direct: build a boat that would travel the furthest distance.

A personal highlight

Team Player award

An engineering challenge that became a lasting memory of building something together.

The aim

Make a design choice the team could build and put to the test in the distance challenge.

The work

I helped build the boat and made a key decision about its design. The project brought together physical construction, a shared deadline and decisions that had to become something real.

What exists

Our boat achieved the furthest distance in the challenge, and I received a Team Player award.

The useful insight

A good design decision matters most when the team can turn it into a working result. This became an early, lasting example of how much I enjoy collaborative engineering.
