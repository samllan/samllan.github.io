    How the map is made

    The snapshots contain PubMed papers on dementia and cognition, with citation data from NIH iCite, and Simple English Wikipedia pages. The comparison includes three summaries checked against the abstracts. Other records link to the source. Structured AI summaries and reader or author input are future directions.

    This project follows on from the exploration patterns in A map of words, the visualisation I built with Nina Sushko at St Andrews.
