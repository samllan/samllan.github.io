# Research limits and next tests

The thesis reports results under its internal evaluation design. It identifies the need for external validation across laboratories, preparation protocols, scanners and patient populations.

An independent study could fix the model and comparison protocol in advance, evaluate on separate data, and report results across sites. A useful follow-up would examine where predictions agree with the supplied references and where changes in image preparation or sample mix alter performance.

For research software, the corresponding work is to record the input version, model setup and inclusion rules alongside each result so another team can tell what was actually compared.

Source — Samuel Llanwarne, *Multi-Scale and Multi-Stage Deep Learning for the Classification of Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma Histology*, University of Leeds, 2026, conclusion and limitations.
